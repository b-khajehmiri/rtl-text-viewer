Privacy Policy for RTL Text Viewer

Last updated: September 15, 2026

RTL Text Viewer is a Chrome extension designed to make selected mixed right-to-left (RTL) and left-to-right (LTR) text easier to read.

Selected Text

When you select text on a webpage and open RTL Text Viewer, the extension accesses the selected text solely to display it in an RTL-formatted reading view.

Selected text is processed locally in your browser. It is not sent to external servers, stored by the developer, sold, or shared with third parties.

Theme Preference

RTL Text Viewer stores your light or dark theme preference locally using Chrome's storage functionality. This preference is used only to restore your chosen appearance when you open the extension again.

Data Collection

RTL Text Viewer does not collect personally identifiable information, browsing history, authentication information, financial information, location information, or analytics data.

Third Parties

RTL Text Viewer does not sell, transfer, or share user data with third parties.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page.
